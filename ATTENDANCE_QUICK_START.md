# Attendance System - Quick Start Guide

## 🚀 What's New

Your attendance system now has:
- ✅ **Selfie-based check-in** for all users
- ✅ **Mobile-friendly interface** that works on phones
- ✅ **Role-based access** - HR sees management features, employees see only their attendance
- ✅ **Real-time status** showing check-in/check-out times

## 📱 For Employees

### How to Mark Attendance

1. **Login** to the system
2. Go to **Dashboard** or **Attendance** page
3. You'll see your attendance card with today's status
4. Click **"Check In"** button (green button)
5. Allow camera access when browser asks
6. Position your face in the camera
7. Click **"Capture"** button
8. Review your selfie
9. Click **"Submit"** to complete check-in
10. At end of day, click **"Check Out"** (red button)

### What You'll See
- ✅ Check-in time
- ✅ Check-out time  
- ✅ Total work hours
- ✅ Your attendance status

### What You WON'T See
- ❌ Other employees' attendance
- ❌ HR management features
- ❌ Payroll, Reports, etc.

## 👔 For HR/Admin Users

### Everything Employees Can Do, PLUS:

### View All Attendance Records
1. Go to **Attendance** page
2. Scroll down to "Attendance Records" section
3. Filter by:
   - **Date**: Select any date
   - **Employee**: Choose specific employee or "All"
4. View detailed table with:
   - Employee name & ID
   - Check-in/check-out times
   - Work hours
   - Attendance status

### Access HR Features
From the sidebar, you can access:
- 👥 **Employees** - Manage employee records
- 🏠 **WFH Requests** - Approve/reject work from home
- 📅 **Leave Requests** - Manage leave applications
- 🎧 **Helpdesk** - Handle support tickets
- 💰 **Payroll** - View payroll data
- 📊 **Reports** - Generate reports

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Camera Access
- Browser will ask for camera permission
- You must **allow** it to take selfie
- Works only on **HTTPS** (secure connection)
- If denied, you can't check-in

### Selfie Requirements
- File size: Max 5MB
- Format: JPEG, PNG, or JPG
- Clear face photo required
- Good lighting recommended

### Check-In Rules
- Can only check-in **once per day**
- Must check-in before check-out
- Selfie required for check-in
- Check-out doesn't need selfie

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Camera Not Working?
1. Check browser permissions
2. Ensure you're on HTTPS
3. Try different browser
4. Restart browser

### Can't Submit Attendance?
1. Check internet connection
2. Verify you captured selfie
3. Try again after few seconds
4. Contact HR if issue persists

### Not Seeing HR Features?
1. Verify your role with HR
2. Logout and login again
3. Clear browser cache
4. Contact system admin

### Mobile Issues?
1. Update your browser
2. Clear browser cache
3. Try different browser
4. Ensure stable internet
